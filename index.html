FAQ

Questions, answered

How long until I can use my sofa or carpet?

Most upholstery is dry in 4–6 hours and carpets in 6–8. Running the AC or a fan speeds it up — helpful with Miami humidity.

Is it safe for kids and pets?

Yes. We use non-toxic, low-residue solutions and rinse thoroughly, so nothing harsh is left behind.

Will every stain come out?

Most do. Some old or set-in stains can only be lightened — we’ll tell you honestly from the photo before you book.

Can you get rid of pet urine smell?

Yes — we treat the source with an enzyme solution that breaks down odor, not just masks it.

Do I need to be home or move furniture?

Someone needs to let us in (condo access is fine). Just clear small items — we move what’s needed and put it back.

How do I pay?

After the job, by card, Apple Pay, Zelle or cash. No deposit to book.
